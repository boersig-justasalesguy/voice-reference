On rent roll-downs, 2019 was our biggest expiration year, and cash rents rolled down 1.3% for the full year, much better than the down high-single digits we initially expected. We retained a little over 80% of expiring leases, right in line with our long-term average. Looking ahead, less than 16% of the portfolio expires in any given year, versus 23% at the start of last year, which sets up a lower churn hurdle. We expect gradual improvement in cash re-leasing spreads into 2020 and beyond. Occupancy did slip 60 basis points in Q4, to 86.8%, on a handful of move-outs, and we expect positive net absorption in 2020. On the broader question about how we work with customers, what we're hearing is demand for a global, fit-for-purpose platform, and that's where we're focused.
