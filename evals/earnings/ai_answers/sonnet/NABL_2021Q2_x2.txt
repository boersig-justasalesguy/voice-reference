Thanks for the question. On the health of the end markets, I'd point to a few signs. We delivered 16% revenue growth in Q2, ahead of the outlook we gave in mid-April. We keep expanding the number of devices managed and secured on the platform, and partners continue to deploy or cross-sell more services powered by it. Demand for security and data protection has been consistently strong. Dollar-based net revenue retention is 110%, and we ended the quarter with 1,647 MSP partners spending over $50,000 of ARR, up 32% year-over-year. Those partners now represent 46% of total ARR, up from 38% a year ago. Those are the indicators we think are most appropriate for growth.

On SMEs, we think we're still early in a large and growing opportunity. According to Frost and Sullivan, our market was approximately $23 billion globally in 2020 and is expected to grow at a 14% CAGR to approximately $44 billion by 2025. SMEs face increasingly sophisticated cyber threats, but unlike larger enterprises, they often don't have the resources and talent to handle mission-critical IT issues, so MSPs are increasingly their trusted partner for IT management and security. We're a trusted partner to those MSPs, and we grow when we add MSP partners, when they add end customers, and when they use more services on our platform. That's the model we're investing behind.
