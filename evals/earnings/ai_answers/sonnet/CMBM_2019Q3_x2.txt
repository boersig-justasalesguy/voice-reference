Thanks for the question. I'll be careful here because we're not giving 2020 guidance today, and I don't want to put a number on it. But let me give you the framework. In Q3, certain pockets were softer than we anticipated, most notably U.S. Federal point-to-point, where specific government programs were delayed, and APAC, where government broadband initiatives are expected to stay slow until calendar 2020. We believe the PTP slowdown is temporary. We also stocked certain products in anticipation of those federal and government-sponsored opportunities, so we're positioned to serve that demand when the programs move.

Beyond the timing items, we think we've built a strong foundation for 2020 and 2021. The drivers we're focused on are the introduction of 60 gigahertz and 28 gigahertz millimeter wave solutions, global Tier 2 and Tier 3 service providers deploying fixed wireless broadband, and Wi-Fi 6 acceleration from the Xirrus acquisition. We also see the $1.5 billion of CAF II funding, to be disbursed over 10 years, as a key driver for 2020. Enterprise Wi-Fi is now over 15% of revenue, up from 7% a year ago. So there are real drivers there. What I'd say is that we have confidence in our future growth, but whether delayed business is a tailwind in 2020 depends on when those programs move, and we'll give you a full view when we guide for the year.
