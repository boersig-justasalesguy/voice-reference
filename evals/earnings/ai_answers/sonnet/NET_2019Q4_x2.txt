Thanks for the question. On ASPs, I don't have a specific number to share. What I can tell you is that we're seeing strong traction with larger customers. Our large customer count, customers with more than $100,000 in annualized billings, was up 76% year-over-year to 550, with a net add of 75 in the quarter. About half of that cohort were existing large customers at the end of 2018, and the other half are a combination of new and expansion customers during 2019. That tells us we can retain and also land and expand. Dollar-based net retention was 112.1%, up 160 basis points year-over-year.

On investments, you're right that our guidance reflects continued investment. Sales and marketing was $43.8 million in Q4, up 57% year-over-year, largely due to head count increases and marketing programs as we add sales capacity and continue to invest in our enterprise go-to-market. So a meaningful part of the investment is building out enterprise sales capacity. We're also seeing leverage in the model, with G&A first, then R&D, and ultimately sales and marketing on a longer-term basis. And we anticipate operating leverage in the second half of fiscal 2020.
