Thanks for the question. You're right that inventory went up in the quarter. It increased by $12.6 million, and inventory days ended September at 180 days, up 23 days from 157 days at the end of June. Turns went to 2.0 from 2.3.

What's behind it is that the buildup is primarily our newer data center products, built at the request of several customers who gave us large forecasts and rollouts for the Cloud. Those rollouts, through a few customers, will extend over three or four quarters, and that should lead to better cash flow in the later quarters through a contraction of inventory. So, to your question on timeframe, think of it as a few quarters of unwinding as those rollouts ramp. I'm not going to give you a specific target for inventory days today. I'd also note that we had a good quarter on cash overall. Cash from operations was $25.7 million, free cash flow was $13.6 million, and DSOs dropped 18 days to 52 days.
