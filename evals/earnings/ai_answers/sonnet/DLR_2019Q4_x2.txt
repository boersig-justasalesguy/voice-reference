On the first part, I appreciate the point. I'd just say that we don't view this as a question of being religious about it. We're focused on being cloud- and carrier-neutral, and that neutrality is a big part of why customers trust us and why our ecosystems are as dense as they are. What we're doing is building solutions for customers on top of that neutral foundation. That's what PlatformDIGITAL is, productized combinations of space, power, cross-connect and management controls. I don't want to speculate beyond that on any specific additional tools, but we'll keep listening to our customers.

On your second question, rent roll-downs and churn, I'd point to what we saw in 2019. It was our all-time high for lease expirations, and we signed over $500 million of renewals, more than 50% above our previous record year, with a weighted-average term of nearly nine years. We retained a little over 80% of expiring leases, right in line with our long-term average, and cash rents rolled down just 1.3%, much better than our initial expectation of down high-single-digits. Going forward, less than 16% of the portfolio expires in any given year, compared to 23% at the beginning of last year, which should set the stage for a lower churn hurdle. We expect continued, gradual improvement in cash re-leasing spreads into 2020 and beyond, and we expect positive net absorption in 2020.
