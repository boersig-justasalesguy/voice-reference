Thanks for the question. I don't have a specific free-to-paid conversion metric to share today. What I can give you is how the funnel looks. We finished the year with nearly 2.6 million total customers, free and paying, up 34% year-over-year, and more than 26 million Internet properties on the platform. Many of those customers don't pay us yet, but they're the top of our marketing, sales and product development funnels. On the paying side, we added over 5,800 paying customers in Q4, an 8% sequential increase, bringing us to over 82,000 paying customers, up 22% year-over-year.

One thing I'd note is that our dollar-based net retention, which was 112.1% in Q4, excludes the benefit of free customers that upgrade to a paid subscription. So the upgrades you're asking about are additional to that. Also, we're shifting to revenue-based KPIs beginning with Q1 2020, with eight quarters of historicals, so I'd encourage you to look at the metrics we report then.
