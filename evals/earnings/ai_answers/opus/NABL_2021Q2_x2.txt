Thanks for the question. I'd say the end markets are healthy, and there are a few signals we watch closely. First, we continue to add new managed devices to the platform, and partners keep deploying and cross-selling more services powered by N-able. Third, net revenue retention ticked up to 110%. And then from a product perspective, demand for security and data protection has been a consistent source of strength, with our SaaS backup and N-able EDR both outpacing overall growth.

On the bigger picture for SMEs, I think this is really the heart of our opportunity. The complexity and sophistication of the cyber threats SMEs face every day have reached record heights, and unlike larger enterprises, SMEs often don't have the resources or the talent to navigate those mission-critical IT issues themselves. So they're increasingly turning to MSPs as their trusted partner for IT management and security. According to Frost & Sullivan, that market was around $23 billion globally in 2020 and is expected to grow at a 14% compounded rate to about $44 billion by 2025. We believe we're still in the early stages of that.

Our job is to be the trusted partner for those MSPs, with a platform that covers monitoring and management, security, data protection and business management in a single pane of glass, and with our partner success organization helping them grow their businesses. When our partners win, we grow, and that's why we're confident in the long-term opportunity.
