Thanks for the question. On the enterprise side, I'd point you to a few data points. Our large customer count, customers with more than $100,000 in annualized billings, grew 76% year-over-year to 550, with 75 net adds in the quarter and 237 over the year. About half of that Q4 cohort were already large customers at the end of 2018, and the other half are a combination of new and expansion customers during 2019. So we're not just landing, we're expanding. You also see it in dollar-based net retention, which improved to 112.1%, up 120 basis points sequentially and 160 basis points year-over-year. We don't disclose an ASP figure, but the deals Matthew walked through, including several multi-year contracts in the $370,000 to $1.1 million per year range, give you a sense of the direction as we move upmarket.

On investments, yes, the guidance reflects that we'll keep investing. The increase in sales and marketing this year was largely driven by head count and marketing programs as we add sales capacity and continue to invest in our enterprise go-to-market, and that continues. We'll also keep investing in R&D to add products and functionality to the platform, and in our global footprint, since international is about 49% of revenue and we see significant potential there. Our plan remains to show operating leverage first in G&A, then R&D, and ultimately in sales and marketing over the longer term, and we anticipate operating leverage in the second half of fiscal 2020.
