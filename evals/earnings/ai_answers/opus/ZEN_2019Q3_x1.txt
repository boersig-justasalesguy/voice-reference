Sure, thanks for the question. The short answer is that the two numbers are measuring different things, so they're going to move differently and I wouldn't read them against each other directly.

The total paid customer account count, which surpassed 150,000 this quarter, is a count of paid accounts. That number is helped by the continued addition of new accounts across all of our regions, and you saw very strong growth in the U.S. and LATAM and good growth in EMEA and APAC. The customer count that underlies the dollar-based net expansion calculation is a different cohort, and it can be affected by things like customers consolidating, including moving onto the Zendesk Suite, which now has more than 5,000 customers. When a customer consolidates onto a bundle or a single account, that can reduce the count without reducing the value of the relationship at all. In many cases it's the opposite.

So which is the better read? For the health of the installed base, I'd focus on the dollar-based net expansion rate itself, which was 116% this quarter. That tells you how much existing customers are growing with us. And for new customer acquisition, the paid customer accounts number is the better indicator. Taken together with 36% revenue growth and RPO up 55% year-over-year, we think both are pointing in the right direction.
