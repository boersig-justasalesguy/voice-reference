Here's the short version. In Relaykit you'd keep one master detection, and per-tenant adaptations would be generated for each client's field names and lookups. Success means a detection fix rolls out to all five in under an hour, with failures explained. Can we get your lead engineer on a 30-minute scoping call this week?

[If yes:] Book it live. [If not yet:] Ask: "What would need to be true for this to be worth a pilot?" and write down the answer.
