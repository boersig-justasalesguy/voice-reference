One useful thing before I go. When we spoke, you mentioned that your coverage reporting for the plant networks was mostly based on what rules exist, not on whether the operational technology telemetry those rules need is actually being collected. To sanity check that, pick five of your OT-related detections and look at when their underlying log sources last sent data. It takes about an hour, and it usually tells you whether a deeper coverage review is worth doing. In my experience, at plants that grew through acquisitions, at least one of those five sources has been quiet for months. Usually a historian server or a firewall was replaced and the forwarding config never got rebuilt. Nobody's fault. It happens when the OT and IT teams have separate change processes.
